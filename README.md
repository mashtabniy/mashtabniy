### ДвижМАКС — Product Case Study

Product case from a MAX hackathon.

**Role:** Product Lead / Product Manager  
**Team:** 4 people  
**Research:** 82 survey responses + 3 interviews  
**Result:** Working MVP

I worked on a chatbot that helps users discover events and find people who are planning to attend the same event.

My responsibilities included:

- user research and analysis of feedback
- JTBD and problem formulation
- product hypothesis development
- MVP scope and requirements
- feature prioritization
- coordination of a 4-person team
- UX texts and product logic
- QA and end-to-end scenario validation

One of the key research insights: **62.2% of respondents had skipped an event they were interested in because they had no one to go with.**
